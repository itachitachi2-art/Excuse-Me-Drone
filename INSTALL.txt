Excuse Me, Drone 1.0.7 - 7 Days to Die 3.4 b10

1. Close the game and back up the old mod folder and customized config.
2. Replace the existing Mods/ExcuseMeDrone folder with ExcuseMeDrone from this ZIP.
   Do not install two copies of the DLL/mod.
3. Restore your preferred Config/ExcuseMeDrone.cfg settings.
   Missing new keys use their defaults. Restart after config changes.
4. Keep the bundled 0_TFP_Harmony installed and disable EAC.

F10: existing drone summon. Also cancels behind lock.
@: toggle behind lock, 3 m behind the owner's body direction by default.
   Toggle OFF returns to Follow and summons in front.
:: toggle the owned drone's inventory within normal interaction range.

Remap BehindLockKey=At and InventoryKey=Colon to single Unity KeyCode names
(e.g. F8 / F9) for other keyboard layouts. Keep all bindings distinct.
BehindLockDistance=3.0 changes the follow distance.
Text fields and unrelated modal windows suppress the new hotkeys.

With a stun baton installed, native stun combat temporarily releases position
lock. After combat, wait at least two seconds and finish any active heal before
returning behind. Manual OFF/F10 cancels that automatic return.
CombatDodge side teleport is suppressed while behind lock is ON, even during
combat suspension. Native approach/retreat, healing and stuck rescue remain.
Healing prerequisites, supplies, range and priority are unchanged.

Behind lock requires single-player/local-host authority. Remote-client and
dedicated multiplayer support is not established.
The maintainer accepted 1.0.7 in-game; this ZIP preserves that tested DLL.
To roll back, close the game and restore your backed-up mod folder.

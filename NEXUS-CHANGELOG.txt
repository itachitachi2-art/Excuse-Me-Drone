Version 1.0.7 - 7 Days to Die 3.4

- Added configurable behind-follow locking (default: @) and drone inventory toggle (default: :).
- Behind-follow defaults to 3 metres and tracks the owner's body direction.
- Toggle off or use F10 to cancel the lock and recall the drone.
- Stun-baton combat temporarily releases the position lock so native combat movement can run.
- Automatically return behind after combat, with a short delay and without interrupting an active heal.
- Suppress the mod's combat sidestep teleport while behind-follow is enabled, including during combat suspension.
- Preserve native stun approach/retreat, healing conditions, normal-mode combat dodge and existing broken-drone recall.
- Inventory access retains the native ownership, interaction-distance and locking rules.
- Rebind the new hotkeys and adjust behind distance in Config/ExcuseMeDrone.cfg.
- Updated for 3.4 b10. Behind-follow requires single-player/local-host authority.

Maintainer accepted 1.0.7 in-game. The distribution uses the same tested DLL.
Dedicated/remote multiplayer remains unverified. This is a prepared package, not a published release.
